Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our consulting and tax services.

How do I register a new private limited company with SECP?

We handle the entire process including name availability check, drafting Memorandum & Articles of Association, digital signatures, and final incorporation certificate issuance from SECP.

What documents are required for annual income tax filing in Pakistan?

You generally need a wealth statement, bank statements up to June 30th, salary certificates or business income details, utility bills, and asset purchase/sale receipts.

Do you provide monthly bookkeeping services for small businesses?

Yes! We offer complete monthly bookkeeping, ledger management, and financial reporting packages tailored to local businesses and e-commerce stores.
